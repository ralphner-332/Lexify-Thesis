<objective>
Build the frontend Post-V3 Final Review Portal, Student & Expert Survey Forms, Research Analytics Dashboard, and Thesis-Ready Academic Report Generator for Phase 4:
1. Post-V3 Final Expert Review Portal (`PostV3FinalReviewPortal.tsx` & `PostV3ReviewWorkspace.tsx`) with unblinded V3 inspection, Likert 1-5 rating scales, error tagging chips, and recommended score input.
2. Student Usability Survey Form (`StudentUsabilitySurvey.tsx`) and Expert Capability Survey Form (`ExpertCapabilitySurvey.tsx`).
3. Survey Manager Dashboard (`SurveyManager.tsx`) for survey results and configuration.
4. Research Analytics Dashboard (`ResearchAnalyticsDashboard.tsx`) visualizing V1 → V2 → V3 Mean Absolute Delta progression, criterion deltas, and score distributions.
5. Research Export & Academic Report Generator (`ResearchExportReportPortal.tsx`) supporting 10 CSV dataset downloads, JSON research bundle export, and printable/downloadable thesis-ready PDF/HTML report.

Purpose: Deliver an end-to-end empirical research platform interface for post-V3 validation, user feedback collection, descriptive analytics, and academic defense reporting.
Output: Integrated React components, views, and routes for Phase 4.
</objective>
